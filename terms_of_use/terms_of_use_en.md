### 1. Scope of Application of the Terms of Use
The federal government, represented by the Procurement Office of the Federal Ministry of the Interior, offers access to information from the Data Service for Public Procurement in Germany via the **Self-Service Portal**. This is particularly for specialist procedure manufacturers. With an account in the **Self-Service Portal**, it is possible to register an administrative user with the authorization to register new technical accounts. These accounts can submit notices via the corresponding technical interface of the Data Service for Public Procurement in Germany and check their status.

The Federal Government, represented by the Procurement Office of the Federal Ministry of the Interior, as operator of the **Self-Service Portal** (hereinafter referred to as the "Operator") provides the **Self-Service Portal** to companies (hereinafter referred to as the "User") via its technical service provider, to which the following terms of use apply.
